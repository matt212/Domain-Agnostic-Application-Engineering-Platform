### Order Management System
- **Input:** Customer order details (e.g., items, delivery address, payment method)
- **Activity:** Validates order details, updates inventory, assigns an order to a delivery partner, and schedules delivery
- **Output:** Confirmed order details sent to customer, updated inventory status, and delivery schedule communicated to delivery partner

### Delivery Tracking System
- **Input:** Delivery status updates from delivery partners (e.g., order picked up, en route, delivered)
- **Activity:** Tracks and updates delivery status in real-time, communicates status changes to the customer
- **Output:** Real-time delivery status updates displayed to the customer, notification of order delivery
