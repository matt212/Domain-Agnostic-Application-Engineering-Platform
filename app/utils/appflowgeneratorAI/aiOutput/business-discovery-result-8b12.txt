============================================================
PRIMARY OBJECTIVE
=================

Construct the most complete BUSINESS MODEL that can be supported
by the supplied Internet research.

The objective is NOT to produce the smallest possible model.

The objective is:

MAXIMUM EVIDENCE-SUPPORTED BUSINESS COVERAGE
WITHOUT INVENTING FACTS.

You must reason across ALL supplied search evidence before producing
the final JSON.

Do not rely on a single search result when multiple sources provide
evidence about the same business capability.

============================================================
EVIDENCE HIERARCHY
==================

Evaluate supplied evidence using the following priority:

1. Official company documentation
2. Official company product/service pages
3. Official company help/support documentation
4. Official filings, reports, policies, and regulatory documents
5. Established industry or business documentation
6. Reputable third-party sources
7. General articles, reviews, blogs, or summaries

Prefer stronger evidence when sources disagree.

Do not treat marketing claims alone as sufficient evidence for a
detailed operational capability when stronger evidence is available.

============================================================
EVIDENCE SYNTHESIS
==================

Before constructing the final model:

1. Identify the primary reference business.
2. Identify the operating model of that business.
3. Identify the complete customer/business lifecycle described by
   the evidence.
4. Identify the major business objects involved in that lifecycle.
5. Identify the major business activities that materially operate
   on those objects.
6. Identify normal lifecycle handling.
7. Identify evidence-supported exception handling.
8. Identify evidence-supported alternative paths.
9. Identify evidence-supported fulfillment variations.
10. Identify evidence-supported post-transaction operations.
11. Cross-check important capabilities against multiple supplied
    sources when possible.
12. Detect obvious lifecycle gaps before producing the final model.

Do NOT stop at the first plausible workflow.

Think through the lifecycle from initiation to completion.

============================================================

For the identified business, examine the evidence for all applicable
parts of the lifecycle:

Include them only when supported by evidence.

============================================================
ACTIVITY MATERIALITY
====================

Do NOT create separate activities merely for:

validation
calculation
authorization
notification
confirmation
status change
simple decision
UI interaction
button click
screen navigation
field entry

When these occur inside a larger business process, represent them as
steps or alternative paths.

============================================================
ACTIVITY CONSOLIDATION
======================

Before creating an Activity, determine whether it is materially
different from an existing Activity.

Do NOT create a separate Activity when the operation is merely:

* a step inside another Activity
* an exception within another Activity
* a validation
* a notification
* a confirmation
* a simple status transition
* a UI operation
* a minor transaction action

Create a separate Activity only when it has a distinct business
purpose and materially distinct lifecycle handling.

Prefer coherent business processes over artificial fragmentation.

For example:

GOOD:

Order Fulfillment
→ Pick items
→ Handle unavailable items
→ Substitute or refund
→ Pack order

Do NOT automatically create:

Item Picking Activity
Item Unavailable Activity
Substitution Activity
Refund Activity

unless the evidence demonstrates that these are independently
managed business operations.

============================================================
END-TO-END COVERAGE
===================

After identifying Activities, mentally trace the business lifecycle.

For each major lifecycle stage ask:

1. What starts this process?
2. Which business object is involved?
3. What business operation occurs?
4. What happens next?
5. What completes the operation?
6. What happens when the normal path cannot continue?
7. Does the next operation logically connect to the previous one?

Do not output this reasoning.

Use it only to prevent incomplete lifecycle models.

============================================================
ORPHAN DETECTION
================

Before producing the final JSON:

Every Business Object MUST be handled by at least one Activity.

Every Business Activity MUST handle at least one Business Object.

Every Activity MUST have a logical relationship to the overall lifecycle.

Do not include isolated objects or isolated activities.

============================================================
EVIDENCE CONFLICTS
==================

When supplied sources disagree:

Prefer higher-quality evidence.

Do not combine contradictory claims into a fictional capability.

Do not assume the more detailed claim is correct simply because it
contains more information.

EVIDENCE GAPS
=============

Do NOT invent missing information.

If a capability is not supported by the supplied evidence, omit it.

However, before omitting an apparently important lifecycle component,
consider whether another supplied source supports it indirectly.

Use ALL supplied evidence before deciding that a capability is absent.

============================================================

===============================

Do not reason:

Instead reason:

"The supplied evidence demonstrates X, therefore X can be included."

=====================================

The PRIMARY REFERENCE BUSINESS is an evidence source for understanding
the business model.

Do not automatically assume every capability of the reference business
belongs to the BUSINESS IDEA.

Only include capabilities supported by the supplied evidence and
relevant to the BUSINESS IDEA.

When multiple search results describe the same capability:

* consolidate duplicate evidence
* prefer authoritative sources
* avoid duplicate business objects
* avoid duplicate activities
* resolve terminology differences
* preserve materially different capabilities

Do not count repeated mentions of the same capability as separate
business activities.

=========================

The objective is:

COMPLETE EVIDENCE-SUPPORTED MODEL

NOT:

MINIMUM MODEL

and NOT:

MAXIMUM ASSUMED MODEL

Include every materially important capability supported by evidence.
